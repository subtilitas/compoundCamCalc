# Compound Cam Calculator

Web app that designs the string track and the power-cable track of a twin-cam
compound bow from a target draw force curve, and exports DXF and STEP files.

- [User guide](user-guide.md): force curve editor, units, undo, saving and
  the meaning of the stats.
- [Model](model.md): bow geometry, cam tracks as support functions, cord
  lengths, the forward model from cam tracks to the draw force curve, the
  limb model and the verification tests with their tolerances.
- [Plan](PLAN.md): model, inputs, outputs, verification and delivery slices.

The app runs at <https://subtilitas.github.io/compoundCamCalc/>.
